# Kygo Style Tropical House Production Guide

## Key Characteristics

### Musical Elements
- **Key**: G Major (most common)
- **BPM**: 100-115 (typically 108)
- **Feel**: Relaxed, sunny, vacation vibes
- **Instruments**: Piano, marimba, steel drums, synth pads

### Signature Sounds
1. **Staccato piano chords**: Short, percussive chords
2. **Marimba/steel drum melodies**: Bright, tropical timbres
3. **Deep house bass**: Sub bass + mid bass layers
4. **Atmospheric pads**: Lush, wide synth pads

## Chord Progressions

### Common Progressions (Key of G)

**I-V-vi-IV (most common):**
```
G(67,71,74) → D(74,78,81) → Em(64,67,71) → C(60,64,67)
```

**vi-IV-I-V:**
```
Em(64,67,71) → C(60,64,67) → G(67,71,74) → D(74,78,81)
```

**I-IV-V-I:**
```
G(67,71,74) → C(60,64,67) → D(74,78,81) → G(67,71,74)
```

## Piano Patterns

### Staccato Chord Pattern (Kygo Style)

**Rhythm: Short-short-long, short-short-long:**
```
Beat:   1    &    2    &    3    &    4    &
Chord:  X    .    X    .    .    .    X    .    X    .    .    .    .    .    .    .
```

**Example (G major):**
```
G3(55, dur:0.25) + B3(59, dur:0.25) + D4(62, dur:0.25) [beat 1]
G3(55, dur:0.25) + B3(59, dur:0.25) + D4(62, dur:0.25) [beat 1.5]
G3(55, dur:0.5) + B3(59, dur:0.5) + D4(62, dur:0.5) [beat 2.5]
```

### Chop Pattern Variation

**Syncopated chop:**
```
Beat:   1    &    2    &    3    &    4    &
Chop:   .    X    .    X    X    .    .    X
```

**Example (C major):**
```
[rest beat 1] E4(64, dur:0.25) [beat 1.5]
[rest] G4(67, dur:0.25) [beat 2.5]
C4(60, dur:0.25) [beat 3]
[rest] E4(64, dur:0.25) [beat 4.5]
```

## Melody Writing

### Tropical Melody Characteristics
- **Range**: G4(67) to D5(74) (one octave)
- **Contour**: Rising, optimistic
- **Rhythm**: Syncopated, bouncy
- **Phrasing**: Short, catchy hooks

### Example Melody (G major)
```
G4(67, dur:0.5) → A4(69, dur:0.25) → B4(71, dur:0.5) → [rest]
D5(74, dur:0.5) → B4(71, dur:0.25) → G4(67, dur:1.0)
```

### Melodic Techniques
1. **Pentatonic base**: Use G major pentatonic (G-A-B-D-E)
2. **Arpeggiation**: Break chords into melody notes
3. **Call and response**: Short call, short answer
4. **Space**: Leave gaps for chords and effects

## Bass Design

### Sub Bass
Low frequency, sine wave:
```
G1(43, dur:1.0, vel:100)
```

### Mid Bass
Saw wave, more harmonics:
```
G2(55, dur:0.5, vel:90)
```

### Bass Pattern
Root on beat 1, fifth on beat 3:
```
Beat:   1    &    2    &    3    &    4    &
Bass:   G1   .    .    .    D2   .    .    .
```

## Arrangement Structure

### Standard Tropical House Structure
1. **Intro** (8 bars): Piano + atmosphere
2. **Verse** (16 bars): Add drums, bass enters
3. **Build-up** (8 bars): Rising energy
4. **Drop** (16 bars): Full arrangement, hook melody
5. **Breakdown** (8 bars): Strip back to piano
6. **Build-up** (8 bars): Rising energy
7. **Drop** (16 bars): Full arrangement
8. **Outro** (8 bars): Fade out

### Energy Curve
```
Intro: 20% → Verse: 40% → Build-up: 40-80% → Drop: 90% → Breakdown: 30% → Build-up: 30-80% → Drop: 90% → Outro: 20%
```

## Sound Design

### Piano Processing
- **Compression**: Medium attack, fast release
- **EQ**: Cut mud (200-400 Hz), boost presence (2-5 kHz)
- **Reverb**: Short decay (0.5-1.0s), medium wet
- **Delay**: Sync to tempo (1/8 note)

### Synth Pad
- **Waveform**: Saw or square, low-pass filtered
- **Filter**: Low-pass at 2-4 kHz, slow LFO
- **Reverb**: Long decay (2-4s), high wet
- **Width**: Stereo widening

### Marimba/Steel Drum
- **Attack**: Fast, percussive
- **Release**: Medium (0.3-0.5s)
- **Reverb**: Medium decay (1-2s)
- **Delay**: Sync to tempo (1/4 note)

## MIDI Note Reference

### Common Tropical Chords (Key of G)
- **G**: 55, 59, 62 (G3, B3, D4)
- **C**: 48, 52, 55 (C3, E3, G3)
- **D**: 50, 54, 57 (D3, F#3, A3)
- **Em**: 52, 55, 59 (E3, G3, B3)

### Velocity Guide
- **Kick**: 90-110
- **Clap**: 80-100
- **Hi-hat**: 60-80
- **Piano chords**: 70-90
- **Melody**: 75-95
- **Bass**: 90-110

### Melody Range
- **Low**: G4(67) - comfortable, relaxed
- **Mid**: B4(71) - bright, optimistic
- **High**: D5(74) - peak, energetic
- **Avoid**: Above E5(76) - too high for tropical feel

### 标志性织体与节奏

- **Staccato 钢琴和弦**：短促弹跳，@0:0.25 + @0.5:0.25 + @0.75:0.5（短-短-长），力度 70–80
- **Marimba/钢鼓旋律**：GM 114，高音区 74–84，八分音符为主、反拍强调
- **反拍 Hi-hat**：只打 @0.5/@1.5/@2.5/@3.5（力度 60–80），是 Tropical 标志
- **低音**：根音+五音长音（d2.0 以上），力度 90–110，不抢节奏
- **Four-on-the-floor**：kick @0/@1/@2/@3（105–120），clap @1/@3（90–110）

### 结构模板

Intro 4 → Loop A ×2（主循环 8+8）→ Loop B（换进行或加 9 音，8）→ Loop A'（回归）→ Outro 4；能量平稳中带跳跃，drop 不是必须。
